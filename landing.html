02Intelligence

From public activity
to commercial intelligence.

Construction activity is public, but it's fragmented — spread across jurisdictions, formats and record systems. CorridorIQ organizes it into information a supplier can actually act on: what's being built, who's building it, and why it matters now.

Built on public permit records and Arizona contractor licensing data. Coverage, contractor detail and freshness vary by jurisdiction and source — we'll review exactly what's available for your territory.

  1. 01

    Permit / project signal

    A public permit record, collected from the jurisdiction's published source on a schedule.

    source: municipal permit
  2. 02

    Project context

    Work type, location and scope where the record provides them — checked for trade relevance.

    trade: plumbing / wet-side
  3. 03

    Company

    Linked to the business named on the record and cross-checked against contractor licensing where possible.

    match: name + license
  4. 04

    Contact / relationship

    Contact details presented as usable only with verification evidence, beside your team's own CRM history.

    contact: verified | not verified
  5. 05

    Why now

    The activity behind the opening, explained in plain language before the first call.

    trigger: new permit activity
  6. 06

    Opportunity

    A short, prioritized list for your team. If nothing passes the checks, the list is empty.

    priority: high · review first

03Procurement Early access

Turn project demand
into a structured request.

Less back-and-forth.
Cleaner material requests.
A direct connection between contractors and the suppliers serving them.

  1. ContractorCreates a material request
  2. ContractorTypes or pastes material lines
  3. ContractorReviews the request
  4. ContractorExplicitly sends it to a connected supplierConfirms exactly what will be shared
  5. SupplierReceives the request
  6. SupplierOpens it
  7. SupplierAcknowledges it
  8. ContractorSees the acknowledgement

Early access. A request carries the contractor's own descriptions and quantities, and goes only to the supplier the contractor selects. Quoting, pricing, availability and ordering stay between contractor and supplier — they are not part of early access.
