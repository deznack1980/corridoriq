# CorridorIQ strategy context (CEO agent v0.2)

Machine-readable source: `company/constitution.json`, `company/maturity.json`, `company/pricing.json`.

## Company

CorridorIQ. Construction intelligence and procurement. Initial market: Phoenix metropolitan area / Arizona construction. Primary users: supply houses and distributors, and contractors.

CorridorIQ is not a permit-lead dashboard. The chain is projects, companies, people, material demand, procurement.

## Products

Product A, supplier intelligence, is the closest path to revenue. External language: multiple public, commercial, and proprietary data signals. Do not publish scoring formulas.

Product B, contractor procurement, is the network product. Early access today is type or paste a material request, structure it, and let a participating supplier receive, open, and acknowledge it when the pilot is running (`pipeline/pilot/materials.py`).

Planned, and not to be sold as live: photo-to-BOM, PDF and spreadsheet ingestion, live inventory, account pricing, automated quoting, quote comparison, payments, checkout, a multi-supplier marketplace, automated compatibility, voice-to-BOM.

AI may classify, OCR, extract, and propose candidates. AI must not fabricate inventory, price, availability, compatibility, or supplier authorization. Supplier systems are authoritative for price, inventory, and availability.

## Economics

Suppliers pay for intelligence. Contractors drive network demand. Procurement connects the two. Contractor basic access stays low-friction or free during network formation. Contractor Pro is a hypothesis.

Internal hypotheses only, not an approved price: founding-partner pilot about $500–$750 per month, preferred Sonoran / founding-partner figure about $750 per month, possible standard after validation about $1,500 per month. Transaction fees are not the default assumption. Publishing a price requires founder approval.

## Flywheel

Supplier intelligence recruits supply houses. Participation and invitations bring contractors. Contractors submit demand. Demand reaches participating suppliers. Procurement signals make the intelligence more valuable. That is a hypothesis about the network, not a measured loop.

Two supplier lanes: make existing customers easier to buy from, and help the supplier find business it does not already have.
