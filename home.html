How it works

From public permit activity to a purchase decision.

Intelligence and procurement are separate capabilities. A supply house can use CorridorIQ intelligence without receiving RFQs, and RFQ participation is a choice each supplier makes.

1

Permits are collected

Public permit records from Phoenix-metro jurisdictions, refreshed on a schedule with source-specific freshness checks.

2

Projects are qualified

Each project is checked for trade relevance and linked to the contractor when the record identifies one.

3

Suppliers act

Sales teams get trade-checked accounts with permit context, and contact details when they're verified.

4

Contractors request quotes

Early access: a contractor turns a bill of materials into one RFQ for suppliers who opt in.

5

Quotes are compared

Responses are compared side by side and the contractor selects a supplier.

Data standards

Built on public records, with verification discipline.

Every project traces back to a public permit record, and contact details carry verification evidence before they're presented as usable.

◎

Real permit records

Projects come from public permit data. Jurisdictions without a verified source are marked pending, never filled in.

✓

Verified before shown

Contact details are shown as usable only after they've been verified for that company.

⚑

Pre-call checks

When something needs a human check before a call, the account says so in plain language.

⌂

Your data stays yours

Your team's CRM notes, call activity, and pipeline are private to your organization.
