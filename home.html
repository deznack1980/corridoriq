How it works

From a project signal to a finished job.

Intelligence and procurement are separate capabilities. A supply house can use CorridorIQ intelligence without receiving material requests, and receiving them is a choice each supplier makes.

1

Project signal

Public permit records, collected on a schedule with source-specific freshness checks.

2

Contractor

Each project is checked for trade relevance and linked to the contractor when the record identifies one.

3

Material need

The contractor builds a material list for the job.

4

Supplier

The contractor sends a request to the supplier they choose.

5

Quote / availability

The supplier responds with its own price, availability, and lead time.

6

Job

Material is ordered and the work gets done.

Steps 3–6 are contractor procurement, in early access and shown in preview with sample data.

Data standards

Built on public records, with verification discipline.

Every project traces back to a public permit record, and contact details carry verification evidence before they're presented as usable.

◎

Real permit records

Projects come from public permit data. Jurisdictions without a verified source are marked pending, never filled in.

✓

Verified before shown

Contact details are shown as usable only after they've been verified for that company.

⚑

Pre-call checks

When something needs a human check before a call, the account says so in plain language.

⌂

Your data stays yours

A supply house's CRM notes and pipeline stay private to that supply house. A contractor's jobs, photos, and material lists stay private to that contractor unless they choose to send something.
