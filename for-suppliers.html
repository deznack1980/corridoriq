How it works

A routine your reps will actually use.

  1. Published records

    CorridorIQ uses permit records published by participating jurisdictions. When a source falls behind, its recommendations are flagged or held back.

  2. Accounts are qualified

    Projects are checked for trade relevance and linked to the contractor's business identity where the record supports it.

  3. Reps call with context

    Each account shows the permit context, what to verify, and the contact when it's verified — and calls are logged in the built-in CRM.

Contractor data stays with the contractor. Your branch sees a material request only when a contractor sends it to you.
