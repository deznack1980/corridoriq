## Who this report is for

Freight brokers, box truck and hotshot operators, LTL carriers, industrial suppliers, and
staffing firms who want to reach Arizona industrial buyers **before** they start shopping for
transportation, supply, or labor partners on the open market.

## Executive Summary

This edition tracked 15 Arizona companies showing recent signals of industrial growth —
new leases, hiring surges, facility expansions, and distributor growth — across the Phoenix
metro, Tucson, and outlying corridors. Six leads scored 85 or higher, indicating strong, timely
opportunity. The West Valley (Tolleson, Goodyear, Buckeye, Avondale, Surprise) continues to be
the highest-density corridor for new industrial activity, driven by distribution and
manufacturing space absorption along I-10 and I-17.

**Top signal category this edition:** New Lease Signed / Facility Expansion Announcement,
together accounting for 8 of 15 leads — a strong indicator that facility footprints are growing
faster than in-house logistics capacity.

## Methodology

CorridorIQ scores each lead on a 0–100 scale weighting signal strength, signal recency,
facility scale, and sector freight-intensity. Full methodology is documented in
[`data/schema.md`](../data/schema.md#how-the-opportunity-score-is-built). Only leads scoring 60+
are included in this report.

## Detailed Profiles

### 1. Tucson Rail Freight Yard Partners — Score 94

- **Location:** Tucson, Pima County
- **Facility:** 300,000 sqft transload facility, ~150 employees
- **Signal:** Manufacturing Expansion announced 2026-03-22, reported via local news
- **Likely need:** Short-haul delivery — rail-to-warehouse drayage
- **Target contact:** Terminal Manager
- **Why it matters:** New transload capacity directly increases short-haul drayage volume
  moving off the rail yard to local warehouses and distributors. This is the earliest possible
  entry point for a carrier relationship, before the facility has a preferred vendor list.

### 2. Sonoran Ridge Distribution — Score 92

- **Location:** Tolleson, Maricopa County
- **Facility:** 285,000 sqft distribution center, ~140 employees
- **Signal:** New lease signed 2026-05-12
- **Likely need:** Short-haul delivery
- **Target contact:** Operations Manager
- **Why it matters:** The tenant took occupancy last month and has not yet contracted local
  carriers — a clean, uncontested window for outreach.

### 3. Papago Building Supply — Score 90

- **Location:** Phoenix, Maricopa County
- **Facility:** 210,000 sqft distribution center, ~110 employees
- **Signal:** Manufacturing expansion tied to West Valley construction demand
- **Likely need:** Short-haul delivery to construction sites
- **Target contact:** Supply Chain Manager
- **Why it matters:** Building materials distributors scale delivery frequency directly with
  local construction starts — a durable, recurring freight need rather than a one-time surge.

## How to use this report

1. Prioritize outreach to the 85+ scored leads first — these represent the shortest window
   before the company locks in a transportation, supply, or staffing vendor.
2. Match your service type to `likely_need` in the data — a hotshot operator should lead with
   overflow/short-haul leads; a staffing firm should lead with `Staffing support` leads.
3. Reference the specific signal (e.g. "I saw you recently signed a lease at...") in first
   outreach — it signals you're tracking their business, not cold-calling from a stale list.
