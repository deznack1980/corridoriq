Less time chasing quotes. More time building.

CorridorIQ isn't a marketplace that resells your project. It's designed as a procurement workspace: you control which project, which materials, and which suppliers see your request. Everything below is in early access and shown in preview with sample data.

What suppliers see

Your request goes only where you send it.

Privacy by design. In the procurement workspace, suppliers see an RFQ only when you choose to send it to them. A supplier sees its own quote, never another supplier's pricing, and suppliers that aren't selected are simply told the RFQ was awarded elsewhere. Supplier participation in RFQs is opt-in and separate from any intelligence products those suppliers use.
