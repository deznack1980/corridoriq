Contractor Pro · $99/month · coming soon

Your field intelligence toolkit.

A paid subscription for contractors and their field teams. It starts with Priority Requests; the field toolkit below is planned, not built — here is what we're designing it to do.

See it.Photograph the existing product or component.
Identify it.Get the likely category and model candidates, and read visible labels.
Verify it.Confirm the missing specs — a measurement, data plate, pipe size, fuel type, or voltage.
Source it.See compatible options from suppliers you're connected to.
How Pro decides — by design
  1. Vision identifies a candidate.
  2. Specifications determine compatibility.
  3. The supplier's system determines availability.
  4. Your supplier account determines price.

A photo suggests a candidate; it never settles compatibility on its own. Regulated and safety-sensitive equipment may always require confirmation by a licensed professional.

Your data stays yours

Suppliers see what you send them. Nothing else.

Requests go where you send them. A supplier sees a material request only when you choose to send it to them, and only the supplier you choose receives it.
Sponsored Contractor Pro seats. In the future a supplier may offer to pay for your Contractor Pro subscription. Sponsorship never gives that supplier access to your jobs, photos, customers, properties, material lists, equipment history, truck stock, searches, or activity.
